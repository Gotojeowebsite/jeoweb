Pirate Defense

By Unblocked Games FreezeNova

Pirate Defense Unblocked throws players into a world of invading pirates, and setting defenses to protect your village from the oncoming threats. Fully equipped with a team of protectors for your village, players will be fighting neck to neck, struggling to protect their village from any and all incoming threats. 

Game Overview

Pirate Defense utilizes the simple concept of setting up defenses to eliminate incoming threats before they invade your village. The game starts off with simple levels consisting of one way in, and one way out for the enemies to pass through. However as the game progresses multiple entry and exit points will be introduced requiring players to protect and keep track of all of them.

Controls

Mouse/Touchpad - Tap, Drag, and Release

Key Features

Tower Placement

Drag and drop different units onto the highlighted towers in the game field to build a strong defense against waves of silly pirates. Once placed, each troop will use its special abilities to help protect the village by stopping the pirates that come close.

Upgrades

Each troop can be upgraded, effectively increasing its firepower to eliminate enemies quicker. Though the firing range, and fire rate remain unaffected which might feel binding, but also keeps the gameplay from becoming redundant and boring. 

Increasing Difficulty

With each level, more entry and exit points will be introduced, requiring players to keep track of all of them at all times. Progression unlocks both troops, and enemy types, with certain enemies only being eliminated by specific troop types. The inclusion of variety keeps the game interesting, and provides a never ending challenge. 

Multiple Regions

Players will be progressing from the lush greenlands, into the barren desert and finally into the secluded forest. These regions serve more than just a different look, since it introduces different enemies, and defense troops, with different abilities. 

Resource Management

Each level starts out with a certain amount of coins available to players. Your starting funds can be used to build up an initial base, though it requires a significant amount of budgeting. Players will need coins to upgrade troops, place more defenses if necessary all while earning coins by eliminating enemies.

FAQs

How many episodes are in Pirate Defense Unblocked?

There are a total of 15 episodes in Pirate Defense Unblocked, of steadily increasing difficulty. 

Can my earned coins be transferred to the next level?

No, your earned coins cannot be transferred to the next level. All coins players earn in a level are reserved for that specific level. 

Can I play offline multiplayer in Pirate Defense?

No, players cannot play offline multiplayer in Pirate Defense since it only provides them with a single player story mode. 

I have accidentally placed the wrong troop, what can I do?

The game has a remove feature, which comes in handy in case players accidentally place the wrong troop, instead of the right one. This simple yet handy remove and replace mechanic helps provide an overall smooth experience.
