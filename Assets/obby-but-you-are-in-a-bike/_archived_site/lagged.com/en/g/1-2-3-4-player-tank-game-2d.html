1 2 3 4 Player Tank Game 2D on Lagged.com

Gather your friends and join the epic 2D tank battle! Each player takes their position, awaiting the start of the endless battle. The goal is to destroy more tanks than your opponents, requiring strategy and skill to emerge victorious.

How to play: *Green Tank: Use 'WASD' to move and 'Q' to fire *Blue Tank: Use 'Arrow Keys' to move and 'P' to fire *Black Tank: Use 'UHJK' to move and 'M' to fire *Red Tank: Use 'FCVB' to move and 'N' to fire
