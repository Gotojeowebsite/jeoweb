Engage in a frantic 1v1 race to hatch the ultimate collection in this competitive egghatching simulator where players navigate eleven rarity tiers and manage a passive income economy that rewards strategic planning. High risk base raids let you steal progress from your rival and turn the tide of the match, adding a daring twist to the fast paced PvP combat. Whether you prefer solo play or local multiplayer, the game blends random excitement with tactical decisionmaking to keep each session fresh. Arcade style challenges and a deep rarity system encourage players to optimize their strategy and master the collection mechanics. Dominate the leaderboard by mastering rarity tiers, timing raids, and perfecting your passive income strategy.

How to play: Use WASD/arrow keys to move, spacebar to jump, shift to run and F/E to interact or touch to play.
