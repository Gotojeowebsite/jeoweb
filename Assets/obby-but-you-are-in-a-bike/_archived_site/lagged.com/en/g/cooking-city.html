Cooking City on Lagged.com

  • 85% like

Master the art of the kitchen in an intense cooking simulator where timing is everything. Players navigate a variety of restaurants, memorize diverse recipes and handle a constant flow of demanding diners. The game combines reflex-based gameplay with strategic kitchen management, challenging you to perfect each dish under pressure. A robust upgrade system lets you unlock new appliances, skills and décor to boost efficiency and serve higher-level customers. With hundreds of levels, you can steadily improve your cooking skills, discover new menu items and compete for top restaurant ratings. This fast-paced restaurant management experience offers endless replay value for fans of cooking games and simulation enthusiasts.
