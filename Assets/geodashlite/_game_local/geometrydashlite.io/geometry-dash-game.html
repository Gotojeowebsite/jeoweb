About Geometry Dash Lite

Introduction to Geometry Dash Lite

Geometry Dash Lite brings a thrilling experience when you drive a geometry on a special road. Tons of dangerous obstacles are added to this road.

Many players recommend Geometry Dash Lite as the most fun game in the running games world. Although this game was released in 2023, its fame spread quickly. It receives the attention of many people and many famous streamers also play this game on their channel. Now, this game is available on our website and you don't need to pay anymore. Your devices are connected to the Internet and you can experience this fun game.

Colorful graphics in this game

The characters in this game are not humans or animals but geometric figures. You will manipulate these geometries to avoid obstacles along the way. This game is designed with many different colors creating an interesting geometric world. The color of the background can even change with each step of the main character's journey. Lighting effects will make the game more vivid than ever. Even though the background or main character is colorful, you won't spend much time downloading game data.

Game music and sound effects

This game is special because each level has its own background music. Some veteran players can even guess the level name as soon as they hear the music of each level. The background music of the levels has one thing in common: the excitement, which stimulates the player's emotions. So, you cannot fully enjoy this fast-paced game if you do not turn on the game sound. The rhythm of the sound also contributes to the success of conquering difficult roads in this game. Please use headphones to experience the vivid sound of this game.

Geometry Dash Lite game rules

This game has very simple gameplay but it requires people's control skills and sharp thinking. Obstacles will constantly appear and you must avoid them skillfully and quickly.

Dodge obstacles by any way

The obstacles in this game are not complicated, but their frequency of appearance is very high. They are blocks and spikes. Regarding blocks, you can use them as platforms to jump on. However, if your character hits the edges of the blocks, it will be destroyed into many pieces. Besides blocks, spikes are extremely dangerous. No matter which side you touch these spikes, your character will be destroyed. In addition, on some levels, dangerous giant cogs appear like sharp spikes. Therefore, you need to control the character to jump over or avoid these dangers. As long as you can maintain a safe character along the way, you can conquer this game.

Collect coins

To pass a level, you need to go to the final portal. Besides this main quest, you also need to complete a side quest. That is to collect 3 coins in each level. The coins will be placed in hard-to-reach locations to challenge players. Collecting coins will not affect the completion of the main mission. Collecting coins only makes the game more difficult. In other words, collecting coins is an optional task. You can demonstrate great skills by catching coins. If you feel this mission is too difficult, you just need to focus on the main mission.

Geometry Dash Lite tips and tricks

Although the gameplay is simple, mastering it is very difficult because the difficulty of the levels will increase. Therefore, you will probably need some of the tips below, whether you are a beginner or a pro in this game.

Be aware after moving through each portal

In each level, there will be different portals. As your character passes through portals, some portals will change their background. You will probably still see your character sliding on the ground or ceilings. Moving the character on the ceiling will be quite difficult for those who are not used to controlling the character because all images will be reversed. This can also be called the phenomenon of gravity change. In addition, sometimes you will also have to face the aerial environment. You need to control your character to fly a UFO in the air.

Enhance your skills with practice mode

To reduce difficulty for beginners, the game has added a practice mode. This mode will help you save data in one level. Specifically, there will be checkpoints automatically created on the roads you have passed. As soon as your character hits an obstacle and is destroyed, you will immediately try again at the last checkpoint. This will not happen if you experience it in normal mode. This training regimen is not only for beginners, but professionals can also take advantage of it. Professional players will easily conquer a level in practice mode and memorize the difficult paths in this level. Then you will have a method to overcome that road in normal mode.

Start from the first level

This game has 15 levels and each of them has a different difficulty level. The developer arranges the level from easy to extremely hard in this game. All these levels are available but you should play this game at the first level. At level 1, you see no support items but you may see some springs in level 2. In the later level, you even have to face different terrain when moving through the portal.

Some other information about Geometry Dash Lite

Besides the attractive gameplay, players are also attracted by some features of this game. You are allowed to change the skins and colors of this character. In addition, the game also offers achievements to give players more goals.

Change colorful skins

The main character has many kinds of shapes which can be a square, robot, UFO, or plane. Each shape has tons of skins and you have many options. All of them are available and you don't need to pay. Besides, this game allows you to customize the color of each skin. Don't use the color of the character as the color of the background. It is difficult to identify the character when the background and character have the same color.

Complete progress bar

The path in a level is shown through a progress bar. You can know the progress of completing a road segment by looking at the progress bar. As soon as the progress bar reaches 100%, you can complete a level. You can choose to disable the progress bar or show it on the screen when controlling the main character.
