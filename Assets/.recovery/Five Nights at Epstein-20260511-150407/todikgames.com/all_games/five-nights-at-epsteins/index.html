Five Nights at Epstein’s

Rating: 4.68/5. From 14 votes.
Please wait...

A locked room, flickering cameras, and five nights that feel far longer than they should. Five Nights at Epstein’s turns surveillance into survival as you monitor threats, manage failing systems, and try to make it to morning without being caught.

The Gameplay

 

Five Nights at Epstein’s is a browser survival horror game inspired by the security-camera mechanics popularized by the Five Nights-style genre. The player is trapped in a small control room and must survive several nights while monitoring camera feeds across the facility. Enemies move through different rooms and vents, and your only defense is awareness and timing. Players switch between cameras, deploy sound distractions to redirect enemies, and close ventilation systems when threats approach. Every night runs from midnight to 6 AM, but each in-game hour lasts only about thirty seconds, forcing constant decisions under pressure. As the nights progress, enemies move faster, systems fail more often, and multitasking becomes essential for survival.

Key Features

 

  • Security camera survival gameplay focused on observation and reaction speed.

  • Compressed night cycle where each hour lasts around 30 seconds, increasing tension.

  • Multiple system mechanics including cameras, vents, and audio distractions.

  • Escalating difficulty with faster enemies and more frequent system failures.

  • Short but intense horror sessions designed for quick browser play.

 

Genre Comparison

 

The structure clearly follows the formula established by the original surveillance horror genre, where the player remains in one room while monitoring enemies through cameras. However, Five Nights at Epstein’s introduces a faster time system and multiple simultaneous control mechanics. Instead of slowly watching enemy movement, players must react quickly as threats approach much faster than in traditional survival horror camera games.

Controls

 

The game uses simple point-and-click controls that are easy to learn but require quick reactions.

  • Mouse Click: Select cameras and activate systems

  • Camera Panel: Switch between surveillance feeds

  • Audio Button: Deploy sound distractions

  • Vent Controls: Open or close ventilation during attacks

  • System Reset: Restore cameras when malfunctions occur
